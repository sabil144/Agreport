  • Egypt gran. $455–480 FOB, Algeria $460–470 FOB, Brazil $430–440 CFR (31 Aug) — Atlantic basin trades at a premium to the Gulf marker.

No fresh print since India tender close; direction is sideways-to-firmer after a two-week rebound. Verify price floors before committing.

2 · DAP / TSP / Phosphate / Sulphur

Still expensive, tentatively softening

  • DAP $880–920 intl (31 Aug); Australian DAP up from ~$670 (early 2026) to $915; MAP $860 (4 Sep). Weekly DAP index $790 (−0.3% w/w).
