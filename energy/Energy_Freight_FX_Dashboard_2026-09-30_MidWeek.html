Executive Insight

Brent's break below $100 is a flow story, not a peace story

Saudi's pipeline is back at half capacity, covert Hormuz transits are running and Washington is draining the SPR. That is enough to pull crude down 11% in a week. It is not enough to reopen the strait: Tehran's offer was rejected, and the White House is pointing to 3 November. For an Australian grain exporter the maths this week is favourable: diesel falling, AUD under 0.70, Pacific freight firm but orderly.
