Hormuz Diesel Squeeze Weekly

By Commodex Intelligence Desk

Diesel remains the first cost shock to hit logistics networks, even if the headline crude market moves more slowly. The issue is not solely the crude oil price; it is the path through distillate and bunker-related freight costs.

Implication

  • A rising diesel curve is a direct margin headwind for transport-heavy supply chains.
  • Freight and bunker pass-through will continue to materialise before broader commodity-price relief arrives.
  • Energy-risk management matters early, while the macro story still evolves.
The key insight is that diesel squeezes the real economy first, while crude prices have a slower and more headline-driven transmission path.
